## 1. The wider Lagos map

**Do not expand the playable city until Chapter 1 and Patrol feel dense.** Surulere is the game. The rest of Lagos is a promise, not a checklist.

### Areas, in order of priority

| Priority | Area | When | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Surulere: Aguda, Ojuelegba, Adelabu, National Stadium, Area Pitch | Now | Home, Chapter 1, Patrol |
| 1 | Third Mainland Bridge + a real UNILAG strip | End of Ch.1 / start of Ch.2 | The JAMB letter has to go somewhere |
| 2 | Yaba / Tejuosho / Sabo / Akoka | Chapter 2 | Markets, students, the next social world |
| 3 | Mushin + a slice of Oshodi | Chapter 2–3 | Harder street, union power, worse heat |
| 4 | Lagos Island: CMS, Marina, Balogun | Chapter 3 | Money, police, spectacle |
| 5 | Ikeja | Later | Airport road, government, radio/TV |
| 6 | Ajegunle / Obalende | Later | Different street codes, not "more Surulere" |
| 7 | Lekki / VI | Last, if ever | Gates, tolls, security. Useful as contrast, deadly as a second open world |

Do not build Lekki until the mainland has a legend. Wide expressways and estates will flatten the movement fantasy.

### What each area should feel like (3–5 things)

**Surulere** (already started; push these harder)
- Compound houses and one-room life behind busy roads
- Bode Thomas food strip and banks
- Adelabu / Aguda markets, generators, queues
- Stadium concrete and dead grandstands
- Ojuelegba as a machine: danfo, bridge, touts, food, never quiet

**Yaba / Tejuosho / UNILAG**
- Tejuosho's rebuilt market halls and electronics
- Student traffic, campus buses, photocopy shops, late food
- Herbert Macaulay grind, CcHub-style glass next to old streets
- Lagoon edge at Akoka: open air after tight Surulere alleys
- LUTH / Idi-Araba as a hospital island of light at night

**Mushin**
- Tighter streets, louder music from shops
- Spare-parts and repair culture
- Cult / area-boy pressure that feels older than the Red Caps
- Markets that do not close, they just change shift
- Less "protector" goodwill; more "who sent you?"

**Oshodi**
- Terminal chaos as a level, not a district
- Under-bridge market and pedestrian conflict
- Flyover as a parkour object
- Union politics in the open
- Easy to enter, hard to leave clean

**Lagos Island (CMS / Marina / Balogun)**
- Packed market lanes, plazas, hawkers in the road
- Colonial / civic buildings against street trade
- Marina air and water after the mainland crush
- Police who look official, not just hungry
- Balogun as stealth-in-a-crowd, not rooftop fantasy

**Ikeja**
- Radio / airport / government corridors
- Better roads, worse cameras
- Alausa as a "you do not belong here in a hoodie" zone
- Computer Village energy if you ever need a tech-crime thread

**Ajegunle**
- Water, wood, density, different skyline
- Local fame travels slower and means more
- A place that should judge a Surulere hero

**Lekki / VI**
- Toll gate as a set-piece
- Gated estates, security phones, empty sidewalks
- Wide asphalt that makes skating loud and visible
- Use for one mission about money, not as a hub

### Landmarks worth making recognisable

Must-read from a rooftop or a chase:
- Ojuelegba flyover and motor park
- National Stadium stands
- Teslim Balogun Stadium
- Adelabu Market
- Third Mainland Bridge
- UNILAG lagoon / gate
- Tejuosho Market
- Adeniran Ogunsanya / Leisure Mall strip

Later, one-shot set pieces:
- Eko Bridge
- CMS bus stop
- National Theatre
- Lekki toll gate
- Marina / CMS waterfront

If a landmark cannot host a chase, a hide, or a conversation, do not spend art on it.

### How the areas connect

Keep traversal diegetic. No fast-travel icon that skips the city.

- **Skate / skitch first:** Funsho Williams, Western Avenue, Ikorodu Road, Third Mainland.
- **Danfo / keke / okada as paid rides:** cheap, public, recognition risk if he just fought.
- **BRT later**, as a clean, timed, high-visibility ride.
- **Bridge runs** as missions, not loading screens.
- **Ferry (Marina–Ikorodu etc.)** only when the Island is in play. One boat scene is enough.
- **Keke-only back roads** as stealth corridors when the expressway is hot.
- Home is always Aguda until Chapter 3. Unlocking a second bed (Kolade, a Yaba student room, a trader's rooftop) is a story reward.

### What each area is for

| Area | Story | Systems |
|---|---|---|
| Aguda | Home, Mama, Tobi, suspicion | Day errands, recognition, sleep/food |
| Ojuelegba | Red Caps, showdown, union | Heat, skitch, patrol density |
| Adelabu | Market, fire, traders | Stealth-in-crowd, daytime crime |
| Stadium | Weapons, lookouts | Vertical stealth |
| Bridge / UNILAG | JAMB, Chapter 2 | Long-distance movement, campus day map |
| Yaba / Tejuosho | Student life, new rumours | Shopping, jobs, a second social circle |
| Mushin / Oshodi | Next gang / union war | Harder patrol, fewer safehouses |
| Island | Money and exposure | High police, low roofs |
| Lekki | One rich-world mission | Cameras, gates, no allies |

### Photos / references

Use Street View and phone photos for:
- Aguda compound yards and water drums

- Adelabu stalls, tarpaulins, generators

## 2. The story

### Bolaji's backstory

Keep it small and unfinished.

- **Home:** Bolaji grew up in one room in Aguda.
- **Father:** gone. Dead, disappeared, or just stopped coming back; do not explain which in Chapter 1.
- **Mama:** made a life from pepper and tomatoes.
- **Bolaji's role:** he became the extra pair of hands early: queues, bills, Tobi, carrying loads, knowing which street to avoid after 9.
- **Running:** he is good at it because that is how a boy without backing moves through Lagos. He learned walls from escaping older boys, not from training.
- **Fighting:** he fights like someone who has lost before and refused to stay down.
- **The cat:** "alley cat" is what the street puts on him. He does not call himself that.

The mystery is not "why is he special?" It is "why does he keep going out when the smart move is sleep and JAMB?" Answer it in play, not in a monologue: he cannot watch the same people get folded twice.

### Origin of the skills

**Natural talent plus street life. Nothing explained.** No bite, no lab, no orisha possession, no secret military uncle in Chapters 1–2.

If anyone ever explains it, it should be wrong. A radio host says he is a cult. A trader says he is a spirit. Mama will say he is her son who does not sleep. All three can stand. No skill-origin cutscene unless the whole tone changes on purpose.

### Chapter 2 and beyond

**Yes, the UNILAG admission still happens.** It is the second life, not a prize screen.

**Chapter 1 end state:**
- Red Caps broken in public, not destroyed.
- Chairman still alive, now hunting the mother.
- JAMB letter in the bag.
- Street rank: "the boy in black".

**Chapter 2 spine:** *Home is no longer the safe map.*
- A neighbour, not the Chairman, almost names him.
- Daytime UNILAG / Yaba: forms, photocopies, campus buses, a life that does not fit the drum.
- Okafor tries to own the rumour: "bring me the boy and the raids stop".
- A cleaner backer sits behind the Chairman: union money or political "security", not a cartoon kingpin.
- The Egúngún returns as the person who understands the costume.
- Tobi finds the drum.
- Mama suspects hard. She does not get the full reveal yet.

**Chapter 3 question:** can he be a student and a night thing, or does one life eat the other?

**Next big villain after the Chairman:** Inspector Okafor as the face, plus an unnamed patron who needs the streets quiet and branded. The Chairman was territory. The next one is the system.

### The Egúngún

Keep him as a recurring rival.

- Do not unmask him in Chapter 1.
- Chapter 2: glimpses. He knows roofs. He knows Bolaji will not kill.
- He is not the Chairman's son and not a demon. He is another poor performer who found a costume that makes people afraid.
- Unmask him only when the name would hurt: a classmate, the workshop boy, someone Mama would feed.
- After that he can still exist. A mask is a job; taking it off does not end the need for it.

### Mama

She should feel it before she knows it.

- **Chapter 1:** questions, the smell of smoke, dawn arrivals, "who was that boy they drew on the pillar?"
- **Chapter 2:** she hides the JAMB letter some nights and leaves it out on others. That is her fight.
- **Full reveal:** late Chapter 2 or Chapter 3, after Tobi is already in danger or after police come to the room.
- **When she finds out,** she does not clap. She bargains: school, or the drum goes in the well.
- Do not use her as a kidnapped prize in Chapter 1. The Chairman's phone line is a threat, not the next cutscene.

### Tobi

Bigger role, slower.

- Not kidnapped in Chapter 1.
- He wants the feeling, not the politics: roofs, the board, the way people talk.
- **First real beat:** he finds the drum or the mask.
- **Second:** he copies a move in daylight and almost gets Bolaji recognised.
- **Kidnapping** is a last-resort Chapter 3 lever. Finding the suit is more interesting and cheaper to build.

### Tone check

Stay **street-real, not torture-real**. PG-13 with adult shadows. The city can be cruel; the game does not have to enjoy it.

**Allowed:**
- Police beating and grabbing
- Axes and machetes as weapons you dodge
- Levy, extortion, raids
- The threat of mob justice
- Shooting at 2+ stars
- A market fire
- Boys Tobi's age in the wrong place

**Not allowed as spectacle:**
- Lingering on tyre-and-petrol
- Sexual violence
- Torture cameras
- Killing named civilians for shock

The Kayode scene should be: the crowd is ready, the petrol is real, you arrive in time. If you fail the timer, fail the mission. Do not play the burning.

### Pidgin and Yoruba

Yes. All street dialogue should be checked by a Lagos ear. Send batches of lines, not the whole script at once.

- **Daytime Aguda:** mixed English, Yoruba, light Pidgin.
- **Night street / Red Caps / conductors:** heavier Pidgin.
- **Radio:** Pidgin performance.
- **Mama:** Yoruba + English. She does not talk like a tout.
- **Tobi:** school English that slips.
- **Police:** official lines in English, street police in Pidgin.
- Do not write decorative Yoruba if it will be spelled wrong. Short, checkable lines only.

## 3. Bolaji's look

**Subtle. Homemade. Readable at night, ordinary enough to hide.**

- **Keep:**
  - Black hoodie, gloves, shorts, socks
  - Two small stitched ears
  - Half mask with amber lenses
  - Small claw tips
  - Board on the back
- **Do not add:** a tail, full fur, printed whiskers, neon eyes, armour plates.
- The cat is in the movement: landing, leap, the shine of the lenses. If the costume explains the metaphor, the metaphor dies.

**Brief for anyone drawing him:**
- Early 20s, lean, not gym-built.
- Lagos night, not comic-book night.
- Hood up, ears only visible as silhouette.
- Amber lenses catch a single streetlight.
- Board worn like a tool, not a brand.
- Day version: cheap shirt, slippers or beat-up sneakers, same face, no mystery.

**Street name:** Chapter 1 keeps **"the boy in black"**. Optional later names, earned and never title-screened too early:
- Ológìnní
- Ologbo
- Black Cat of Aguda (radio talk)
- The Aguda Cat

Let rank and radio invent the cat name after "Street rumour".

**Later suit:** sewn by Mama, after she knows or after she has decided not to ask. The upgrade is love and durability, not power.
- Same black base.
- Better stitching.
- A hidden radio pocket.
- Reinforced gloves.
- One domestic detail only she would add.
- No cape, no crest, no logo.

## 4. Gameplay decisions

### Patrol mode

**Keep:**
- Beatings
- Levy points
- Phone snatchers
- Police checkpoints / roger
- Armed robbery car chase
- Rooftop rings
- Skate challenge
- Unexplored streets
- Food / home when needs are low

**Add:**
- Breakdown shakedown on the bridge or at a junction
- Generator theft from a night stall
- One-chance danfo
- Okada snatch
- Blackout looting
- Flooded keke / child on a median
- Second-collection levy after market close
- Crowd around an accident with pickpockets inside it
- Street football about to become a stabbing
- Cable thieves on a transformer
- A false "protector" collecting money in his name

Patrol should mix fight, chase, stealth, help and traverse. If every ping is a brawl, the loop dies.

### Progression

**Split it:**
- **Respect** unlocks access, not superpowers: warnings, a rooftop mattress, a trader who lies for him, cheaper food, a conductor who will let him skitch, useful Patrol Board info.
- **Money** buys life: food, sleep medicine/plaster, board parts, bus fare, a better radio battery, gloves.
- **Moves** stay mostly street-earned and early. Do not gate "lands on feet". Maybe gate Cat Sweep, long Pounce and Street Sense upgrades so the first hours stay readable.
- **Street rank** is reputation copy, not a skill tree: Nobody → Street rumour → The boy in black → Surulere's protector → Legend of Lagos. Legend changes how crowds and police react, not a new fatality.

### Difficulty

"Dangerous if sloppy", not boss-souls.
- Early fights are 2–3 boys, with readable counters.
- Axes and machetes stay uncounterable.
- Police heat stays: 1 star = chase and grab, 2+ = shots. But give alleys and roofs that actually work.
- The recognition meter should scare players more than health.
- If combat is easy, add interruptions (a second ring, a keke in the road, a civilian in the swing), not bigger health bars.

### Death / failure

**Keep: beaten, wake up robbed, gear scattered, heat still warm.**

Add:
- The wake-up location changes with heat: gutter, clinic bench, Kolade's floor.
- A missing board or mask becomes a short recovery loop.
- Mama's suspicion ticks up if he comes home wrecked.
- Never a "you died" splash that breaks the night.
- On a critical story-mission failure: crawl, hide, and retry from a nearby roof, not from the door every time.

### Vehicles

**He never drives.** He skates, skitches, rides danfo/keke/okada as a passenger, rides in a boot or truck bed if a mission needs it. At most he hijacks a ride as a passenger, then jumps off.

### Multiplayer

Not now. If ever: co-op patrol before story co-op, roof/skate races with ghost lines, no shared identity reveal.

## 6. Art and assets

**Stay code-built for the city. Buy or borrow a few hero assets.**

- **Free / cheap assets:**
  - Danfo, keke, okada
  - A rigged male base (Bolaji, then reskinned enemies)
  - Mixamo animation cycles
  - Kenney / Quaternius only as block-in, then painted over
- **Code-built:** buildings, roads, markets, wires, signage, flood, UI, radio, newspapers, most props.
- **Characters:**
  - One rigged Bolaji once movement is locked, with day and night skins on the same rig.
  - Custom reads for Mama, Tobi, Scorpion, the Egúngún and the Chairman.
  - Everyone else shares 3–4 bodies.
- **Vehicles:** real-ish danfo / keke / okada first; they're the highest visual payoff per model.
- **Rule:** no asset that cannot be replaced in a week gets to define a system.

## 7. Technical and practical

- **Performance:** three settings, Low aimed at integrated graphics.
  - **Low:** no crowd beyond 20, simpler lights.
  - **Medium:** the default.
  - **High:** more stalls, more traffic, longer view.
  - Blackouts double as a performance tool.
- **Controller:** Xbox and PlayStation labels; detect the pad and swap icons. No extra face buttons.
- **Where people will play:** a local build and a private link (GitHub Pages / Netlify / Vercel) now → itch.io once Patrol + 3 story missions are completable → Steam only much later.
- **Mobile:** not the target. If ever, a smaller spinoff.
- **Git:** commit now, one repo, commit each playable version, push to a private GitHub repo, and zip a weekly backup.
- **Credits:** "Created by [Name] · Surulere / Lagos · Additional thanks: Mama's generation of traders, testers, anyone who checks Pidgin". Decide the name or handle before itch.io.
