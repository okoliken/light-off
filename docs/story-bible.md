# LIGHT-OFF: Story Bible

> Reworked 2026-10-14. The ten-mission story (the General, Okafor, the Hunter, the boys in black) is gone.
> Anything not written here is not part of the game.

## Bolaji
- An adult living alone in a rented room in Aguda, Surulere. Rent is due every week.
- **By day:** a rider for **SwiftDrop Dispatch**. His work clothes are the orange SwiftDrop shirt and cap. He's paid weekly, per delivery, and he can lose the job.
- **By night:** the suit. A black spider suit with red webbing on the mask, a red spider on the chest, an open black hoodie with red lining, loose shorts and red high-tops. It's hidden in the water drum at home, or carried in his backpack (U to change where nobody can see).
- Nobody knows why he can do what he does. He doesn't either.

## What he can do (in the suit)
| Power | How | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Wall-crawl** | Run or jump at a wall | Already there from the cat days. |
| **Web swing** | In the air: hold **E** | Webs the nearest roof corner or NEPA pole top ahead and off to one side, swings through an arc, lets go at the top. Keep E held to web the next one. Drains energy slowly. |
| **Web zip** | On the ground: **E** facing a building | Straight up to that rooftop. Costs 3 energy. |
| **Venom strike** | **B** | A bio-electric burst. Everyone within 3.6 m is dropped and stunned. Costs 15 energy, 2.5 s cooldown. |
| **Camouflage** | **Z** (toggle) | He fades to a shimmer. The police and the street cannot see him. Drains 3 energy a second; ends on its own when he fights, goes down, goes indoors or runs out of energy. |

E still hangs onto a moving vehicle when one is right next to him, and still rides a NEPA wire.

## The game: Patrol, day and night
- Lagos, day or night, doing whatever you want. No missions, no story characters, no special gangs.
- Ordinary street life only: pickpockets, bag snatchers, armed robbers, one chance danfos. People to help: return what was stolen, stop a robbery.
- The police chase the suit on sight. Camouflage is how he loses them.
- Side jobs, rooftop runs, transport, the malls, Coach Ayo.
- No progression to worry about. Just your own play.

## Places
Surulere (home in Aguda, Ojuelegba, Adelabu Market, the National Stadium), Ladipo, Lagos Island (Marina jetty, Broad Street, City Hall), and the bridges and ferry between them.
