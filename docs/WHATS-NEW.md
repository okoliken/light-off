# What's new: the free-roam update

Notes for the next post: what changed since the first public build, and what to film for each device.

## In one line
Light-Off is now an open Lagos you can just play: pick any place on the map and get there however you like (skate, okada, keke, danfo, or a stolen bike). It's playable on a laptop, a phone, a tablet or with a PS/Xbox controller, and a hands-on tutorial teaches you as you go.

## The big changes

### Play on any device
- **Phones and tablets:** on-screen controls. A joystick appears under your left thumb (push it all the way to run), you drag the right side to look, and there are buttons for Jump, Hit, Dodge, Pounce, Board, Grab and more.
- **Controllers:** PlayStation and Xbox pads work, and the prompts switch to that pad's buttons.
- **Laptop/desktop:** keyboard and mouse, with a clear **Pause (Esc) · Controls (H) · Map (M)** bar always on screen.
- Phones start on Low graphics and use a smaller HUD. In portrait, the game asks you to turn the phone sideways.

### Learn by doing: a first-steps tutorial
- New players get 11 short tasks: walk, look, run, jump, get on the board, skate fast, ollie, flip, strap the board back on, open the map, pause.
- Each task is shown in the controls of the device you're using: keys on a laptop, buttons on a phone, pad buttons on a controller. It moves on only once you've done it.
- You can skip it or replay it from the pause menu. **H** shows the controls card any time.

### A new map
- A night-mode city atlas:
  - building footprints with shadows, glowing streets and the lagoon;
  - Surulere, Yaba and Mushin each tinted differently;
  - live traffic lights moving on the roads;
  - a glowing animated route;
  - a pulsing "you are here" marker with a view cone;
  - a compass and a scale bar.
- **Search any place** ("Where to?"): home, markets, malls, bus stops, food spots, landmarks.
- **Pick a place, then pick how to get there:**
  - **Skate/walk:** free, with a guide trail.
  - **Okada:** fast and pricier; hail one where you stand.
  - **Keke:** cheaper and slower.
  - **Danfo:** cheapest; from the nearest bus stop.
- Drivers refuse if police are after you. Works with touch (drag, pinch, tap) and mouse.

### Skating
- Higher ollies (the faster you go, the higher you pop).
- **Body flips:** press Jump again in the air for a **backflip**, **frontflip** or **360 spin**.
- **Board tricks:** kickflip, heelflip, 360 flip, shove-it.
- **Belly-board:** at speed, lie flat on the board, face down, hands gripping the front, ready to pop up.
- A smoother skating sound: a low rumble of wheels and pavement clicks instead of a hiss.

### Free roam, and no "hero" pressure
- No forced missions. Day rolls into night and back again, and you go wherever you want.
- **Steal an okada:** pull the rider off ("Ole! My okada!") and ride it. Witnesses call the police.

### Little Lagos details
- **NEPA wires everywhere:** wooden poles, sagging lines and the tangle of service drops to every house (Mushin is the worst).
- **NEPA crews:** a PHCN pickup pulls up at a transformer near you, a man climbs the ladder, sparks fly, and the street goes dark. Neighbours beg: "Abeg, my pikin dey write exam tomorrow!" You can **"settle" the NEPA man (₦1,000)** to leave your light alone.
- **Walk into the malls** (Tejuosho, E-Centre):
  - an air-conditioned hall with a fountain and an escalator;
  - an electricals shop (torchlight), a pharmacy, Mama Chi Provisions, and a food court with jollof and small chops;
  - security at the door: "Small boy, you dey buy or you dey look?";
  - open 9 AM to 9 PM.
