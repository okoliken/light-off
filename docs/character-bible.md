| **Street name** 💡 | Options: **"Light-Off"** (he's gone once NEPA takes light), **"Bj Ghost"**, **"Oju-Ina"** ("eye of fire"). |
| **Age** ✅ | Early 20s (about 21 at the start) |
| **From** 💡 | Surulere, Lagos, in a face-me-I-face-you compound near Ojuelegba |

**The meaning of his name** 💡: *Bọ́lájí* is Yoruba, usually taken to mean "one who wakes up to wealth." He was born into nothing, and the irony is part of who he is. His whole story is about deciding what "wealth" really means: money, or the people around him.

## 2. Personality

**Core** ✅: bold, daring, a risk-taker.

**Around the core** 💡:
- **Confident to the point of cocky.** He talks trash mid-chase, and taunts police in Pidgin before disappearing.
- **Street-smart, not book-smart (yet).** He reads people, traffic and danger instantly. UNILAG humbles him, because a lecture hall can't be outrun.
- **Loyal.** He takes risks for himself freely, but never gambles with his people.
- **Funny.** His humor is a shield. He jokes most when he's scared.

**His flaw** 💡: his boldness is also his weakness. He takes on jobs that are too big, pushes his luck, and doesn't know when to stop. The story makes him pay for it at least once.

**What he fears** 💡: ending up like the men he grew up around: stuck, bitter, or dead young. UNILAG is his way out, and the streets keep pulling him back.

**Voice** 💡: he switches between Pidgin on the street, Yoruba with family, and careful English at school. How he talks shows which world he's in.

---

## 3. Backstory 💡

- **Early life:** raised by his mother, a market trader, in a single room with a younger sibling. His father left or died (to decide).
- **The streets:** from about 12 he hustles: hawking in traffic, carrying loads at the market, running errands for anyone who pays. He learns every shortcut, back alley and bus route.
- **The skateboard:** he builds his first board at 14 from a broken wardrobe door and trolley wheels from the market, to hawk faster between cars. Skating becomes his escape, then his skill.
- **The turn:** one incident sets him on the Robin Hood path, for example watching agberos or corrupt police shake down his mother's stall. He decides to take back what's being stolen from his people.
- **UNILAG:** after several attempts at JAMB (the university entrance exam) while hustling, he finally gets admission at 21. It's the proudest day of his mother's life, and the start of his double life.

## 4. Look

### Day look (street / campus) 💡
- Lean, wiry build: a runner and skater, not a gym body
- Short hair, maybe a shaved design or small scar in his eyebrow
- Worn sneakers, joggers, an oversized secondhand jersey (a Super Eagles or Premier League top from the Yaba market)
- At UNILAG he tries to look neat: an ironed shirt and his best trousers, and it shows he's trying hard

### Early night look: Suit v1 ✅
Simple and all black, made to disappear into the dark. He isn't a "hero" yet, just a boy who doesn't want to be recognized.
- **Hoodie:** all black, hood up
- **Gloves:** black
- **Shorts:** black
- **Socks:** black
- **Skateboard:** strapped to his back. He unclips it to skate and straps it back on to run or climb (see section 5).

💡 Proposed details: a black face mask or neck gaiter pulled up under the hood, and one small personal detail (a bead bracelet, a stitched patch) so he's recognizable in silhouette.

### Suit rule ✅
**His costume is always black.** Any later upgrade stays black; no Ankara or other patterned attire (decided 2026-09-28).

### No web shooters ✅
Removed (decided 2026-09-28). Jumping spiders don't spin webs to catch prey, and a street kid couldn't afford to build web shooters anyway.

### Suit rules ✅ (2026-09-30)
Subtle, homemade, readable at night.
- **Never:** tail, fur, printed whiskers, neon eyes, armour.
- **v2 (sewn by Mama):** better stitching, a hidden radio pocket, reinforced gloves, one domestic detail. Love and durability, not power.
- **He never drives.**

### Cat-like street kid ✅ (decided 2026-09-29, replaces the jumping-spider idea)
No spider connection. Bolaji is a street kid who is exceptionally good at running, climbing and slipping away, and fights like an alley cat. The abilities stay the same, with cat names:
- **Cat leap:** hold jump to crouch and spring up to about 5 m.
- **Pounce:** onto an enemy up to 16 m away.
- **Lands on his feet:** big drops barely hurt him.
- **Silent takedowns:** from behind or in the dark.
- **Cat Sweep:** when surrounded.
- **Danger sense:** he feels attacks coming.
- **Suit v1:** all black, with cat ears stitched onto the hood, a half mask with amber lenses that catch light like a cat's eyes, and small claw tips on the gloves.

### Acrobatics (on foot): street parkour
Learned by running from trouble across compound walls, market stalls and rooftops. Not polished gymnastics, just survival.
- **Vaults:** over stalls, bike bars, gutters and low walls, without breaking speed
- **Wall-run and wall-jump:** up to rooftops and across narrow alleys
- **Climbing:** on burglar-proof bars, water tanks and zinc roofs
- **Rolls:** landing from a height without injury
- **Slides:** under trailers, parked danfos and half-closed gates
- **Roof hops:** across the tight gaps between buildings

### Board tricks
Style that's also useful:
- **Ollie, kickflip, grind:** basic traversal and style points
- **Rail and ledge grinds:** on gutters, road dividers, bridge rails
- **Wallride:** along compound walls
- **Board catch:** kick the board up from the ground and catch it or strap it on in one motion
- **Tricks off vehicles:** launch off a danfo roof or ramp off a parked car

### Fighting style
Rooted in his culture and his life:
- **Gidigbo base:** Yoruba traditional wrestling, which he may have picked up as a kid in the compound. Throws, sweeps and grapples use the enemy's weight against them.
- **Street boxing:** fast jabs and dirty hits he learned surviving agberos.
- **Board as a weapon:** swing it, block with it, throw it and call it back, or slam it down on someone's feet.
- **Environment:** pull a stall shade over someone, kick a bucket, tip over a generator, slam a gate.
- **Hit-and-run:** a quick combo and then gone, back onto the board or up a wall. The game should reward escaping, not only winning.

### Limits (these keep him human)
- He can't tank hits. Two or three and he's down, so dodging matters most.
- Big enemies (the Chairman's heavies) can't be thrown. He has to trip them, use the environment, or run.
- Guns change everything. If police draw, the only move is to escape.
- Fighting draws attention. The longer a fight goes on, the more police and agberos arrive.

### Upgrades (as the story goes on)
- Learns proper Gidigbo from an elder, unlocking stronger throws
- Suit v2 adds padding (can take one more hit) and gadget options
- New board tricks unlock through skating practice
