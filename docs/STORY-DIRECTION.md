# Story direction (October 2026)

Where the game is going, in the creator's words, tidied up. It replaces the student / Mama framing in the older docs for anything new.

## Who Bolaji is now
- **By day: an errand/delivery runner.** He works for a small dispatch company in Lagos. He picks up parcels, electronics, documents and food at the office, and gets them to people across the city on his board, a bike, danfos and kekes. He has workmates who do the same job. Real money, a real boss, a real phone that keeps ringing.
- **By night: the boy in black.** The suit lets him go where a delivery boy can't, to the people who hurt others. He isn't a clean "hero": he steals, he runs, he fights dirty. Lagos just happens to be on his side more often than the police are.
- **No more Mama/student storyline.** No Mama sending him on errands, no UNILAG student angle. The job is the day life.

## The pressure
- **The job is on the line.** Customers report late or missing deliveries. Every distraction (a fight, a chase, a side quest) costs time, and the boss notices.
- **The copycat.** Another masked vigilante works the same streets, but he's fiercer and crueller. The police can't tell them apart, so whatever the copycat does lands on Bolaji.
- **The police.** To them, a boy in a black suit is a criminal. Any sighting means a chase, with no pity. Day to day they run checkpoints and collect "roger", and they raid areas looking for "Yahoo boys".

## How missions will feel (after the world is right)
- A day's work is a **mission**: "Deliver 15 items today." Collect them at the office, plan the route on the map, go.
- On the way: side quests, trouble, the copycat's mess, police raids. Choosing to help someone means a late parcel.
- Nights: the suit, the people who hurt others, and the Patron's network behind them.

## World first (the creator's order)
Make the default free roam feel natural and alive first, then layer the missions on top.
