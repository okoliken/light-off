# Story direction (October 2026)

Where the game is going, in the creator's words, tidied up. It replaces the student / Mama framing in the older docs for anything new.

## Who Bolaji is now
- **By day: an errand/delivery runner.** He works for a small dispatch company in Lagos. He picks up parcels, electronics, documents and food at the office, and gets them to people across the city on his board, a bike, danfos and kekes. He has workmates who do the same job. Real money, a real boss, a real phone that keeps ringing.
- **By night: the boy in black.** The suit lets him go where a delivery boy can't, to the people who hurt others. He isn't a clean "hero": he steals, he runs, he fights dirty. Lagos just happens to be on his side more often than the police are.
- **No more Mama/student storyline.** No Mama sending him on errands, no UNILAG student angle. The job is the day life.

## The pressure
- **The job is on the line.** Customers report late or missing deliveries. Every distraction (a fight, a chase, a side quest) costs time, and the boss notices.
- **The copycat.** Another masked vigilante works the same streets, but he's fiercer and crueller. The police can't tell them apart, so whatever the copycat does lands on Bolaji.
- **The police.** To them, a boy in a black suit is a criminal. Any sighting means a chase, with no pity. Day to day they run checkpoints and collect "roger", and they raid areas looking for "Yahoo boys".

## How missions will feel (after the world is right)
- A day's work is a **mission**: "Deliver 15 items today." Collect them at the office, plan the route on the map, go.
- On the way: side quests, trouble, the copycat's mess, police raids. Choosing to help someone means a late parcel.
- Nights: the suit, the people who hurt others, and the Patron's network behind them.

## World first (the creator's order)
Make the default free roam feel natural and alive first, then layer the missions on top.

## Done in this pass (on the local `freeroam` branch)
- **Checkpoints** now have 4 or 5 officers: two in the road and the rest loafing by the table. They still stop vehicles and collect roger.
- **Raids:** a "Police · Special Squad" van parks up, three young men stand against the wall with their hands up, and the officers go through their phones. "Bring your phone. Unlock am. Now!" / "Officer, I be student! Na school laptop!"
- **No pity:** police who see the suit give chase at once.
- **Ride conversations:** the driver and Bolaji talk about fuel prices, NEPA, Third Mainland traffic, the Super Eagles, japa, the boy in black, roger, the rain, delivery work, Big Brother and garri prices.
- **Street talk** near stalls, bus stops and crowds: traders, conductors, a preacher, a man on the phone saying "I don reach Ojuelegba".
- **Bus stops:** danfos, BRTs and kekes pull in, and the conductor shouts the route ("CMS! Obalende! Oya oya!").
- **Ojuelegba:** traffic now runs both ways over the flyover. Underneath there's a danfo park, hawkers and a crowd.
- **Third Mainland** traffic now drives down onto Lagos Island and round the cross roads, then heads back. No more U-turning on the bridge.
- **Lagos Island:** new landmarks are Tinubu Square's fountain, the Central Mosque, a CMS BRT terminal and a Marina waterfront. Idumota, Balogun and the Adeniji Adele interchange now show up in map search.
- **Ferry:** LagFerry runs between Ebute-Metta Jetty and Marina Jetty for ₦500, with seated passengers, the driver shouting about life jackets, a wake behind the boat and chatter on the way. The map now suggests the ferry when you pick somewhere across the water.
- **Okada for hire:** on your okada, stop at a bus stop and press F to carry a passenger. They name the place, you name the price, and you get paid when you drop them off. Crash and you get nothing.

## Done in the second pass (local)
- **Lagos Island rebuilt:**
  - Isale Eko's pastel family houses;
  - Broad Street and Marina bank towers (First Bank, Union Bank, Access);
  - rooftop billboards (MTN, Glo, Indomie, Peak, GTBank, Dangote, OPay, Milo, "Eko o ni baje"…) and ground-floor shop signs;
  - the Idumota clock tower with an Eyo statue, Iga Idunganran (the Oba's palace), the Cathedral Church of Christ, City Hall, Tafawa Balewa Square with its white horses, and Obalende motor park;
  - Island food stalls (buka, suya, puff-puff, boli, corn) and proper area names.
- **Crowds follow you:** people far off-screen move to the streets and hangouts near you, so the Island and every other district have people.
- **Ferry:** a matte teal and yellow boat instead of shiny white.
- **"The Boy in Black" tutorial:** starts the first time you go out in the suit. 13 steps:
  - climb, get onto a roof, cat drop, wall-run, flip, cat leap, Street Sense, skitch;
  - then three agberos jump you: strike, counter, launch, pounce, and finish them (with a Cat Sweep when they crowd you).
  - Replay it from the pause menu.
