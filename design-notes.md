# Design Notes: Ideas to Develop

> Status 2026-09-28 (v0.3): items 1, 2, 5 and 6 have a first version in the game. Chapter 1 (The Red Caps) is built.

Captured from the user on 2026-09-28. These are ideas and open questions, not decisions yet. We'll shape them together in the story session.

## 1. Bolaji has to live (survival needs)
- **Hunger:** he needs to eat. For example, buy food from a bukka, suya spot or roadside akara seller, or Mama feeds him at home.
- **Rest:** he needs sleep.
- **Condition:** when he's hungry or tired he's less effective than at 100%. Possible effects: slower, weaker hits, less Street Sense, slower health regeneration, harder climbing.
- **Going home to rest:** some scenarios require it.
- *Open questions:* how fast do needs drain? Is there a day/night clock? What does "100%" unlock?

## 2. Secret identity
- **Going home:** on the way back he must make sure nobody sees where he's going, so nobody learns who he is.
- **Possible mechanics:** a "being watched / followed" meter, losing a tail, changing out of the suit, and entering home only when unseen.
- *Open questions:* what happens if someone finds out? (Mama, Tobi, the Chairman, the police?)

## 3. Wanted by more than the police
- **Who's after him:** the police, agberos and other street figures.
- **Idea:** separate "wanted" levels per faction, and each faction hunts him differently.

## 4. Rival vigilantes
- **Who they are:** others who can do what he does, and some are *more* acrobatic than him. They're trying to do what he does, but not the right way.
- **The conflict:** Bolaji has to face up to them.
- *Open questions:* who are they, and what are their motives? Are they rivals, villains, or future allies?

## 5. Group fights
- **Scale:** fights against 3–5 people at the same time.
- **Needs:** a better crowd-combat system (targeting, counters or dodges, enemies taking turns, crowd control).

## 6. Damage system
- **In the moment:** show when he's hurt and how badly, both visibly on his body and in how he moves.
- **Lasting marks:** carry damage into the future (for example, a torn suit, bruises, a limp) until he heals or repairs.

## 7. Wayfinding: done in v0.2
- A guide arrow points along the road route to wherever help is needed.
- The minimap shows the GPS route.
- Street Sense shows a glowing trail on the ground.

## 8. Later
- Multiplayer (not now: single-player first).
- Story expansion: the user will bring more storyline details.

## Story direction (from the user, 2026-09-28)
- **Where he starts:** Bolaji starts as a street kid living with his mother, **before** UNILAG admission. UNILAG comes in a later chapter.
- **Chapter 1, The Red Caps:** a cult of agberos terrorizing Surulere. Their base is under the well-known bridge; I'm assuming **Ojuelegba Bridge**, so confirm with the user.
- **Missions:** they start from his house at night while Mama sleeps. Story and side missions run side by side.
- **Paused for now:** car chases and the UNILAG race.
- **Later ideas:**
  - rescuing people from burning buildings
  - hiding in places like a bank, where people protect him
  - making the map feel like real Lagos places
- **Rival vigilantes:** more acrobatic than him, but doing it the wrong way. Still to design.
