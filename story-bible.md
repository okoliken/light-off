## Theme ✅
*Bọ́lájí* means "one who wakes up to wealth." Born into nothing, he spends the game answering one question: **is wealth money, or your people?** Every villain chose money. Bolaji keeps choosing people.

## How the game plays ✅ (user direction)
- **GTA-style open world across Lagos.** Surulere is only the start; key areas get built out over time.
- **Day:**
  - Bolaji in his everyday clothes, with his skateboard, and **no suit**.
  - Errands for Mama; discovering places.
  - **News that inspires the night's mission or patrol:** newspapers, radio, street gossip.
- **Night:** Mama asleep, the black suit, missions and patrols. Home before dawn, and never lead anyone home.
- **Transport:** **enter any transport** (danfo, keke, okada, BRT, ferry), pay the fare, and drop off at a destination.
- **Lagos texture:** not every road is good. Broken asphalt, dirt roads, potholes, flooding.
- **Living:** hunger, energy, injuries, rest at home (built).

## Locked decisions (2026-09-30, see PART-B-DECISIONS.md)
- **Backstory:** one room in Aguda; the father is gone (never explained in Ch.1); he was Mama's extra pair of hands; he learned walls escaping older boys. The real mystery: why he keeps going out when the smart move is sleep and JAMB.
- **Skills:** natural talent + street life. Never explained; every in-world explanation is wrong (cult, spirit, "my son who does not sleep").
- **Map order:**
  1. Surulere
  2. Third Mainland + UNILAG strip
  3. Yaba / Tejuosho
  4. Mushin / Oshodi
  5. Lagos Island
  6. Never Lekki as a hub
- **Ch.1 end state:** the Red Caps are broken in public, the Chairman is alive and hunting the mother, and the JAMB letter is in the bag.
- **Ch.2 spine:** "home is no longer the safe map".
  - A neighbour almost names him.
  - UNILAG / Yaba daytime life.
  - Okafor + a quiet patron as the next villains.
  - Tobi finds the drum.
  - Mama suspects hard.
- **Egúngún:** a recurring mirror, unmasked late, and only when the name would hurt.
- **Mama:** suspects in Ch.1, knows late Ch.2 or Ch.3; she bargains, she doesn't clap. Never a kidnap prize in Ch.1.
- **Tobi:** finds the drum or mask first, then copies a move in daylight. A kidnapping is a last-resort Ch.3 lever.
- **Tone:** street-real, not torture-real. Mob justice is a threat, never shown burning.
- **Name:** "the boy in black" in Ch.1. A cat name (Ológìnní...) only if the street/radio invents it later.

## Chapters
### 1 · The Red Caps ✅ (built, v0.8: ten connected missions; full list in NEEDS.md)
Each mission ends with a scene that points at the next one. Missions start from the door at home, at night; they are never forced.
1. **Baba Ade:** stop the beating and return the cash box. Baba Ade says the Red Caps pay the police at the Ojuelegba checkpoint.
2. **Roger:** watch the Red Caps' bagman hand Inspector Okafor an envelope at the checkpoint, take it, and lose the police. The envelope lists the payroll and the three traders held under the bridge.
3. **Under the Bridge:** free Iya Sade, Mama Chioma and Oga Emeka. Iya Sade warns about a masked "Egúngún" doing mob justice.
4. **The Masquerade:** a timed race to save Kayode (accused of stealing a phone) from a tyre and petrol. Fight the Egúngún's knife boys, then the acrobatic Egúngún, then chase him; he escapes. Kayode says the Red Caps are asking at Baba Kolade's workshop.
5. **The Chairman's Message:** rescue Baba Kolade at the motor park. Scorpion flees wounded, promising every Red Cap will gather under Ojuelegba.
6. **Showdown at Ojuelegba:** three waves (knives and sticks; axes and machetes; Scorpion's crew). Tear down the Red Caps' flag and paint **SURULERE IS NOT FOR SALE**. The Chairman: "Find out who he is. Find his mother."
- **The radio ✅:** Bolaji's pocket radio (N) and the radio on the window sill at home are how he hears the news. The next story mission unlocks when he hears it on the air (newspapers and street gossip work too). Tobi no longer gives leads.
- **Daytime missions ✅ (no mask):** *Levy in Broad Daylight* (pick the collector's pocket at Adelabu Market), *Bag Snatchers of Bode Thomas*, *Tobi's School Fees*. By day, in his own clothes, fighting and cat moves fill **EYES ON YOU**; if it fills he is recognised: the mission fails, Mama's suspicion jumps, and the police want to question him. At night, in the suit, nobody knows his face.
- **The rival:** the Egúngún, an acrobatic masked vigilante who does the right thing the wrong way (protection money, mob justice).
- **The police:** checkpoints collect "roger" from every danfo. After *Roger*, officers who see the boy in black at night chase him on sight.

### 2 · Admission 💡
- **UNILAG admission:** Mama's joy, and a **₦150,000 acceptance fee** they can't pay.
- **Double life:** lectures by day, missions by night; grades suffer if he skips class.
- **Ada:** a journalism student with the late-night podcast *Eko Nights*, investigating "the boy in black." Ally, maybe more.
- **The Egúngún:** a masked rival crew, *more acrobatic* than Bolaji, dispensing **jungle justice**, burning thieves in the street. The city confuses Bolaji with them. He must stop them and **rescue their victims from the fire**.
- **Temptation:** the Chairman quietly offers to pay his school fees.

### 3 · Third Mainland 💡
- **Framed:** Inspector Okafor frames "the boy in black" for murder, and a full police manhunt follows.
- **The bank:** hiding in a bank during a raid, sheltered by the market women and bank staff he once helped.
- **The bigger crime:** the Chairman's real business is **fuel diversion**: stolen tankers, fake scarcity.
- **Set piece:** a **tanker fire** in go-slow traffic on Third Mainland Bridge. Rescue the trapped passengers.
- **Tobi**, copying his big brother, gets taken.

### 4 · Ojuelegba 💡
- **The truth about the Chairman** (tied to the origin once it's decided).
- **Mama's reveal:** Mama knew. The suspicion meter pays off: she finds the hoodie and **sews him a proper suit, still black**.
- **The finale:** the Red Caps, the Egúngún and corrupt police at Ojuelegba Bridge.
- **The choice:** hand the Chairman to **Sergeant Adaeze** with Ada's evidence, or let the crowd have him (the jungle justice he fought all game).
