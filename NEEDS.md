# Part A: Where the game is right now (v0.8)

## The character: Bolaji

- **Who:** a street kid in his early 20s from Aguda, Surulere. Lives in one room with **Mama** (a pepper and tomato trader) and his little brother **Tobi** (about 12).
- **By day:** his own clothes, errands for Mama, hustling, and hearing the news.
- **By night:** when Mama is asleep, the suit comes out of the water drum.
- **What he is:** not a superhero. A street kid who is **very good at running, climbing and slipping away**, and who fights like an alley cat. Where his skills come from is **left a mystery** for now.
- **The suit (v1, cat-styled):**
  - Black hoodie, black gloves, black shorts, black socks, and his homemade skateboard strapped to his back.
  - Two stitched **cat ears** on the hood.
  - A black **half mask** with amber lenses that catch the light like a cat's eyes at night.
  - Small **claw tips** on the gloves.
- **What the street calls him:** "the boy in black". An open question: should it become a cat name? (See Part B.)
- **The big rule:** nobody can know who he is. Not even Mama.

**Survival:**
- **Health:** hits leave **injury** that caps his max health until he eats and sleeps.
- **Knockdowns:** if he's knocked down he can get up; a second knockdown is critical and he has to **crawl away**.
- **Needs:** **hunger** and **energy** drop over time; low means slower and weaker, with no flips.
- **Police heat:** up to 3 stars. Police chase and grab him, and at 2+ stars they shoot.
- **Red Caps interest:** how hard the gang is looking for him.
- **Mama's suspicion:** noise at home, sleeping in the suit, coming home at dawn, being recognised by day.
- **Eyes on you (daytime only):** in his own clothes, fighting and cat moves in front of people fill the meter. When it's full he's **recognised**: day missions fail, Mama hears, and the police come looking.

## Chapter 1: The Red Caps (10 story missions)

The **Red Caps** are an agbero cult ruling Surulere from under Ojuelegba Bridge, led by **the Chairman**, with **Scorpion** as his machete enforcer and **Inspector Okafor** of the police on their payroll.

How missions work:
- Every mission is unlocked by hearing about it: the **radio** (N), newspapers or street gossip.
- It starts from Bolaji's door at night.
- It opens with a **cutscene**.
- It ends with him getting home **without being followed**.
- Each ending scene points to the next mission.
- Missions are optional: you can always patrol instead.

1. **Baba Ade.** The Red Caps are beating the old shoemaker for the third night. Stop them and return his cash box.
   - *Lead out:* Baba Ade says the Red Caps pay the police at the Ojuelegba checkpoint.
2. **Roger.** Watch the Red Caps' bagman hand Inspector Okafor an envelope at the police checkpoint, take it, and lose the police.
   - *Lead out:* the envelope lists the payroll, and three traders held under the bridge.
3. **Under the Bridge.** Raid the Red Caps' camp under the Ojuelegba flyover and free Iya Sade, Mama Chioma and Oga Emeka.
   - *Lead out:* every Thursday, one black car carries all of Surulere's levy money to the Chairman.
4. **The Levy Run** *(new)*. Chase the levy car on your board, skitch onto it, smash it until the driver crashes, beat the crew, and give ₦60,000 back to the market women.
   - *Lead out:* a masked man in Egúngún cloth is calling himself Surulere's "protector".
5. **The Masquerade.** Race a timer to save Kayode, accused of stealing a phone, from a crowd with a tyre and petrol. Fight the Egúngún's boys, fight **the Egúngún** (an acrobat who flips away from plain punches), then chase him; he escapes.
   - *Lead out:* Kayode saw the Red Caps storing crates of weapons in the National Stadium.
6. **The Stadium** *(new)*. Stealth: sneak into the old National Stadium, take out five lookouts with **silent takedowns** (if one sees you, backup arrives), and wreck the weapon crates.
   - *Lead out:* a notebook under the last crate names Baba Kolade as the mechanic who fixes the boy in black's board.
7. **The Chairman's Message.** The Red Caps drag Baba Kolade to the motor park. Fight through with axes and machetes coming at you, beat **Scorpion**, and free Kolade.
   - *Lead out:* Scorpion flees, letting slip that Okafor's police are coming to raid Aguda.
8. **Black Maria** *(new)*. On your own street, police are loading young men into a van to beat a name out of them. Free Dayo, Chuks and Segun before the van leaves. Officers see what's in front of them, so pull a transformer fuse to kill the lights.
   - *Lead out:* the Egúngún plans to burn Adelabu Market, where Red Caps sleep among the traders.
9. **Fire at Adelabu** *(new)*. Put out four fires before the market goes up, beat the Egúngún's boys, and beat **the Egúngún** a second time.
   - *Lead out:* he yields: "Friday. Ojuelegba. Every Red Cap in Lagos. I will not stop you."
10. **Showdown at Ojuelegba.** Three waves under the flyover (knives and sticks; axes and machetes; Scorpion's crew). Tear down the Red Caps' flag and spray-paint **SURULERE IS NOT FOR SALE** on the pillar.
    - *Ending:* on the phone, the Chairman says, "Find out who he is. Find his mother." Then the JAMB letter waits in Mama's handbag.

## Daytime missions (no mask)

These are unlocked on the daytime radio, one at a time. They fail if he's **recognised**.

- **Levy in Broad Daylight:** Red Caps shaking down Iya Sade at noon. Pick the collector's pocket from behind and walk away calmly.
- **Bag Snatchers of Bode Thomas:** two thieves run in opposite directions; only one has the bag. No pouncing in front of the crowd.
- **Tobi's School Fees:** area boys robbing Tobi at the school gate. Fists and counters only.

## Patrol mode (endless, separate from the story)

Pick **Patrol** on the main menu. It has its own save and never touches the story.

- Nights roll on forever: dawn just starts the next night.
- The **Patrol Board (J)** lists everything happening near you, nearest and most urgent first. Pick one to get a waypoint:
  - Red Caps **beating** someone
  - **Armed robbery:** chase the getaway car, skitch on, smash it, fight the armed robbers, return the takings
  - **Phone snatchers**
  - **Red Cap levy points**
  - **Police checkpoints** holding "roger" money
  - **Rooftop runs:** glowing rings across the roofs against the clock
  - **Third Mainland go-slow robbers:** the bridge jams, and robbers dressed as hawkers (trays on their heads) rob the stuck cars, then bolt for the railing to drop into waiting Makoko canoes. Catch them before they jump.
  - **Third Mainland sprint:** skate or skitch from the Surulere end to the Adeniji Adele interchange on Lagos Island. Gold under 62 s, best time saved.
  - **Skate challenge** at the Area Pitch
  - Places you haven't **explored**
  - Food when you're hungry, home when you're tired
- **Street rank** grows with respect: Nobody → Street rumour → The boy in black → Surulere's protector → Legend of Lagos.
- The radio sends you calls about crimes nearby.

# Part C: Expansion plan (from your "give it more shape" suggestion)

**The idea:** missions feel short because they're *objectives*, not *nights*. So we keep the 10-mission spine and make each one a whole night, with living days in between. We don't add more numbered missions yet.

**Every mission follows one template:**

| Beat | What happens |
|---|---|
| Hook | Radio, newspaper, gossip, a trader |
| Look | Daytime recon in plain clothes |
| Do | The job at night |
| Turn | Something the briefing didn't mention |
| Run | The getaway |
| Morning after | The city and Mama react |
